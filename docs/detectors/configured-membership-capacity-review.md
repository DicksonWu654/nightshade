# Configured membership capacity review

Availability: **opt-in** with `--detectors configured-membership-capacity-review`.
Severity: **info**. Confidence: **high** for the represented state/constraint contradiction.

The independent observer invokes the constructor then a membership-bearing entry
with fresh inputs and exact carried storage. It records assertion prefixes,
including prefixes rejected by ordinary execution. Current collection capacity
upper bounds and enforced pairwise key inequalities can prove that required
memberships are inconsistent immediately after that represented construction.

This does not establish permanent unusability, concrete invocation feasibility,
or native cryptographic validity. Later enrollment or other configuration can
change storage. No constructor or unknown entry storage supplies an empty-set
assumption. The rule never promotes sequence failures to universal
`impossible-assertion` findings.

Only exact immutable receiver/key identities and required Boolean constraints
participate. Wildcard address equality, stale saved membership results and
unknown collection replacements provide no capacity proof. Missing evidence,
unsupported relations/effects or exhausted budgets diagnose. Conservative
upper-bound tracking is enabled only for this observation; ordinary collection
snapshots and defaults remain unchanged. Unrelated entries receive resolved
structural absence coverage; independent baseline gaps remain explicit.

Ordinary registry dispatch stays inert without the optional constructor-boundary
facts. Selected scans request the independent observer and retain its missing
evidence and budget diagnostics.

A policy that intentionally requires more distinct members than the configured
capacity may produce this informational finding by design. Later configuration
can change the result; the observation is not a permanent-usability proof or a
claim that the constructor can be deployed or invoked successfully.
